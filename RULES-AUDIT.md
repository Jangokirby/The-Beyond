# Rules audit: the game vs the 2025 rulebook (TBRPG_Layout-2025)

Checked against the full book: Playing the Game (pp.13-14), Character Creation and Backgrounds (pp.15-24),
Magic (pp.25-28), Weapons and Armor (pp.29-31), Equipment, Wealth, Services and Housing (pp.32-37),
Environment (p.38), Investigating (pp.39-40), Combat, CRs, Statuses, Zones (pp.41-46), Healing, Injuries,
Death, Disorders, Mutations (pp.47-50), Advancement (p.51), Monsters (pp.53-58), Artifacts and Patrons.

## Already matching the book (no change)
- Core ranks 4/3/1 and Aspect spreads 4-3-2-1 / 3-2-1 / 2-1; Aspect cap level+2, Special Aspect level+3.
- Healing Pools 5 + 5 × Core Stat. ER 7 + Agility + armour; BR = armour + Durability; MR = Willpower; SR = Morality.
- Word Pool 5 × (Occult + Faith + Ancient); Potency sets string length and active Duration spells.
- 2 actions a turn; attack = 2d6 + Aspect + weapon type vs ER; damage = attack total − resistance.
  12 is a Critical, 2 fails and gives a counter-attack.
- CR scale Easy 7, Medium 10, Challenging 14, Dangerous 18, Hopeless 20.
- Damage types (Health, Sanity, Stability, Elemental, Corrupted, Spell).
- Statuses: Bleed 3 × 3 rounds, Burning 4 a round until put out, Fear, Terrified, Soul Severed, Frozen 2 turns,
  Crippled, Befuddled, Blind −4, Suffocation, Disease, Exhausted, Forgetfulness, Possessed, Beyond Touched.
- Lighting (Shadow −2, Bright/Dark = Blind, Blinding/Pitch = Blind and one fewer action) and the vision types.
- Speed order and Interruptions; Zone control (twice the numbers, CR above Medium counts extra).
- Every Zone Modifier, Weather severity, Fate Points (one a mission, extra uses cost Noticed).
- Injury (regain 10 and a free Flee) or the Death Die (7+ regains half of every pool; below, marked for death
  and the GM's secret d6). All 21 injuries, 17 Mental Disorders, the Mutation table and 10 Mutation Traits.
- Level every 2 missions: +1 Core, +2 Aspect points and one of the five benefits; retirement after 3 level-ups.
- Every weapon type, weapon and armour Modifier and Specialty, Wealth Class, Service, Housing Modifier,
  Monster type, Monster Modifier, Legendary Modifier, the Difficulty table, all 16 Beyond Artifacts and the deities
  (numbers compared automatically against the book text: no differences).
- Size table numbers.

## Book wording that conflicts with itself (the game's reading)
- **Ties.** Attacks say the total must "exceed" ER, but the Accurate example (ER 9, Accurate, "a 7, 8 or 9 hits")
  counts a tie as a hit. The game counts ties as successes everywhere.
- **Death Die.** "Rolling a 7 or above … survives. Rolling below 9 marks the character for death." The game uses 7.
- **Health.** p.22 says Health = Body × 5; p.23 says pools start at 5 and add 5 per Core point. The game uses 5 + 5 × Core
  (a Body 1 agent has 10 Health), the reading in the Healing Pools section.

## House rules (labelled in the game, unchanged)
- Origin languages and the cost of extra languages (the book: native language, plus one more at IQ above 3).
- The corkboard theory and working contacts for Favors.
- The old 5/8/12/16/18 CR table, available in Settings for tables that began with the older draft.
- Construct Attack +3 (above).
