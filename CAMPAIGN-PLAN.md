# Campaign plan: one deep, long campaign per era (and more to follow)

Rule of the book: an era is a *period*, not a plot. Agents may have been present at the great events of the age, but the
jobs are not always tied to them. So every era gets two kinds of campaign: one that touches the age's big moments, and one
that has nothing to do with them. Each campaign is 5 to 8 parts, one villain or mystery that grows, choices that carry
forward (arcSet), and recurring people who remember you. Every part meets the floor in CASE-STANDARD.md (clues, searches,
limited interviews, secondary goals, several ways to win, tiered endings).

Already written (3 parts each): The Pilgrim Road (1321, Old Order), The Lamp-Black Cycle (1889, Gaslight),
Dead Air (1994 to 1996, Late Century), Grey Market (2079 to 2081, Present).

## Engine work the campaigns need
* Campaign hub tab (done) with the recurring cast, the villain's file and "what you chose".
* Carry-over: wounds, favours and named allies between parts (partly done with arc flags).
* Reputation per agency across parts, so the villain reacts to what the team did.
* Between-part downtime scenes (travel, a letter, a dream) so a campaign feels like a life, not a list.

* Any people, places or events from the books that must appear.
