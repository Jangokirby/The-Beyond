Rule used for old records: recorded in 1925 or earlier (public domain in the US since 2026, and long out of
protection in the EU and UK), and the song's writers died before 1956 (so the composition is free in the EU too).
The 78s come from the Internet Archive's Great 78 Project; the transfers keep the surface noise on purpose.
Trimmed to four minutes, volume-matched, mono.
